# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01  
**Last Updated:** 2026-11-03

Nyxora Sync ("we", "us", "our") operates the website at https://nyxora-sync.tech. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our service.

## Information We Collect

### Account Data (Optional)
If you choose to sign in with GitHub:
- GitHub username, display name, avatar URL
- Email address (if public on GitHub or provided)
- We do **not** receive your GitHub password or private data

### Progress Data
- Completed lessons, XP, levels, streaks
- Notes and bookmarks you create
- Earned badges and certificates
- Stored locally in your browser by default
- Synced to our database only if you sign in

### Community Data (If you participate)
- Display name and bio (you choose what to share)
- Comments on lessons and projects
- Projects submitted to the showcase
- Follow relationships (who you follow)
- Reports you submit or receive

### Newsletter Data (If you subscribe)
- Email address (double opt-in confirmed)
- Confirmation timestamp
- Unsubscribe timestamp (if applicable)

### Contact Form Data (If you contact us)
- Name, email, subject, message
- Stored for admin response purposes

### Technical Data (Automatic)
- IP address (for rate limiting, security)
- Browser user agent
- Referrer
- Pages visited
- **No cookies** for analytics or tracking
- **No third-party analytics** (Google Analytics, etc.)

## How We Use Your Data

| Data Type | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| Account | Authentication, sync progress across devices |
| Progress | Show your progress, award badges, generate certificates |
| Community | Enable discussions, showcase, profiles, notifications |
| Newsletter | Send curriculum updates (only after double opt-in) |
| Contact | Respond to your inquiry |
| Technical | Rate limiting, abuse prevention, security |

## Data Sharing

We **do not sell** your personal data.

We share data only:
- **With your consent** (public profile, showcase projects)
- **With maintainers** (admin access to messages, reports, newsletter list)
- **As required by law** (legal process, safety)

Third-party services we use:
- **GitHub OAuth** — Authentication only
- **Vercel** — Hosting (processes IP, logs for security/performance)
- **Neon (PostgreSQL)** — Database hosting
- **Cloudflare Workers** — Request proxy (no data stored)
- **Resend/SMTP** — Transactional emails (confirmation, notifications)

## Data Retention

| Data | Retention |
|------|-----------|
| Account | Until you delete your account |
| Progress | Until you delete your account |
| Community content | Until you delete it or account |
| Newsletter email | Until you unsubscribe (then deleted) |
| Contact messages | 1 year after last reply |
| Technical logs | 30 days (Vercel default) |

## Your Rights

You can:
- **Access** your data — View in Settings or request export
- **Rectify** — Edit profile, progress, newsletter preferences
- **Erase** — Delete account (Settings → Delete Account)
- **Restrict** — Make profile private, disable progress sync
- **Portability** — Request JSON export of your data
- **Object** — Unsubscribe from newsletter, disable notifications

## Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us.

## Security

- All traffic over HTTPS (TLS 1.2+)
- Passwords never stored (OAuth only)
- Database credentials rotated regularly
- Admin access restricted to maintainers
- Rate limiting on all mutable endpoints
- CSP headers on all pages

## International Transfers

Data processed in the United States (Vercel `iad1` region, Neon `us-east-1`). By using the service, you consent to this transfer.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced via:
- Notification on the site
- Newsletter (if subscribed)
- GitHub repository commits
