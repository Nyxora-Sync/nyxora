The discovery call is where you prove you're a partner, not a pair of hands. Most freelancers treat it as "let me hear your requirements." Pros treat it as **qualification + diagnosis + trust-building** in 15 minutes.

This lesson gives you a repeatable framework to run discovery calls that close.

## The 15-Minute Discovery Call Structure

```
0:00–1:00   │  Rapport & Frame Setting
1:00–8:00   │  Diagnosis (BANT + Technical)
8:00–13:00  │  Solution Sketch & Scope
13:00–15:00 │  Next Steps & Close
```

---

### Minute 0–1: Frame Setting
**Say exactly this:**
> "Thanks for the time. I've read the job post and checked your [site/app/repo]. I have a few questions to understand if we're a fit — then I'll share how I'd approach it. Fair?"

**Why this works:**
- Shows preparation (you read their stuff)
- Sets expectation: *you're qualifying them too*
- Positions you as a consultant, not an order-taker

---

### Minute 1–8: Diagnosis (The BANT+T Framework)

| Area | Questions | What You're Listening For |
|------|-----------|---------------------------|
| **Budget** | "What's the budget range approved for this phase?"<br />"Is this a one-off project or part of a larger initiative?" | Real number or range. "We'll see" = no budget. |
| **Authority** | "Who else is involved in the decision?"<br />"Will you be the day-to-day point of contact?" | Direct access to decision-maker. Committees = delays. |
| **Need** | "What's the business impact if this *doesn't* get done this quarter?"<br />"What have you tried already?" | Urgency + pain. "Nice to have" = low close rate. |
| **Timeline** | "What's driving the deadline?"<br />"When do you need this in users' hands?" | External trigger (launch, investor demo, compliance) = real. |
| **Technical** (+T) | "What's the current stack?"<br />"Any existing code, infra, or team I'll integrate with?"<br />"Any hard constraints (compliance, legacy, geography)?" | Scope clarity. "We'll figure it out" = scope creep risk. |

**Golden Rule**: You talk 20%, they talk 80%. Take notes. Use their exact words in your proposal.

---

### Minute 8–13: Solution Sketch
**Don't present a full proposal.** Sketch the approach verbally:

> "Based on what you've told me, here's how I'd approach it:
>
> **Phase 1 (Week 1–2):** [Core foundation — auth, DB, CI/CD]
> **Phase 2 (Week 3–4):** [Main feature — the outcome they care about]
> **Phase 3 (Week 5):** [Polish, tests, staging review, deploy]
>
> **Rough investment:** Professional tier at $X — includes 30-day support, 2 revision rounds, staging review before production.
>
> **What's excluded:** [Design from scratch, content migration, third-party license costs]
>
> Does this direction feel right, or should we adjust scope?"

**Why this works:**
- Gives a number *before* the proposal (no sticker shock)
- Lets them self-select out if budget doesn't match
- Shows you think in phases, not "I'll code it"

---

### Minute 13–15: Next Steps & Close

**If they're bought in:**
> "Great. I'll send a 2-page scope document by EOD tomorrow with exact deliverables, timeline, and payment schedule (50/40/10). Once you sign, we pick a start date. Sound good?"

**If they need time:**
> "Totally. I'll send the scope doc anyway so you have the details. When's a good time to follow up — early next week?"

**If they're not a fit:**
> "Honestly, this sounds like it needs [X] which isn't my specialty. I know someone who does that well — happy to intro. For [your niche], I'm your person."

## Red Flags to Watch For

| Signal | Response |
|--------|----------|
| "We don't have a budget yet" | "No problem — let's pause until there's an approved range. I'll hold a slot for 2 weeks." |
| "Can you start today?" | "I don't start without a signed scope and 50% deposit. Earliest start: [date 1 week out]." |
| "We'll pay after launch" | "I only work on 50/40/10 milestones. The 40% at staging demo means you see it working before paying more." |
| "My cousin/nephew knows React" | "If you have internal capacity, use it. I'm here when you need senior-level delivery with accountability." |
| "We need unlimited revisions" | "My tiers include 2 revision rounds. Beyond that, we scope a change order at $X/hr. This protects both of us." |

## Remote Call Logistics (International Clients)

| Tool | Setup |
|------|-------|
| **Video** | Google Meet (no install), Zoom, or Whereby (browser) |
| **Scheduling** | Calendly (free) — set to *your* timezone, shows in *theirs* |
| **Time Zones** | Always confirm: "That's 2 PM UTC / 9 AM EST / 7 PM IST — correct?" |
| **Recording** | Ask permission: "Mind if I record for my notes? I'll delete after." |
| **Connection Backup** | Phone number / WhatsApp ready if video fails |

## Exercise: Run 3 Practice Discovery Calls This Week

- **Step 1**: Find 3 practice partners — fellow learners, dev friends, or post in Nyxora community
- **Step 2**: Give them a fake brief (use real job posts you saved)
- **Step 3**: Run the 15-min framework — record it (Loom/Zoom)
- **Step 4**: Review: Did you talk under 20%? Did you get BANT+T? Did you close with next steps?
- **Step 5**: Iterate — rewrite your questions based on what felt awkward

## Key Takeaways
- **Discovery = qualification, not requirements gathering**
- **15 minutes is enough** — if they need more, schedule a paid workshop
- **Their words > your jargon** — use their language in the proposal
- **Price on the call** — prevents ghosting after proposal
- **Calendly link = professional** — removes "when are you free?" ping-pong
